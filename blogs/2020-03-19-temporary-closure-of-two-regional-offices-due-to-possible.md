March 19, 2020 Effective immediately, the National Labor Relations Board’s offices in Cleveland and New Orleans will be closed temporarily while an employee in each of these offices is tested for COVID-19. The Agency is taking this step to ensure the health and safety of our employees and the public. Under today’s announcement, all employees in these offices will telework until notified otherwise.
