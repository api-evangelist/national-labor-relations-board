March 24, 2020 Washington D.C. – The National Labor Relations Board announces that, to facilitate the resolution of legal challenges, it is postponing the effective date of its final rule modifying the Agency’s Representation Case Procedures from April 16, 2020 to May 31, 2020. The notice will be published later in the week.
