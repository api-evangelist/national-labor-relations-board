March 15, 2020 Effective immediately, the National Labor Relations Board’s offices in Manhattan, Detroit, and Chicago will be closed temporarily while an employee in each of these offices is tested for COVID-19. The Agency is taking this step to ensure the health and safety of our employees and the public. Telework-ready employees in these offices were previously scheduled to telework on Monday, March 16.
